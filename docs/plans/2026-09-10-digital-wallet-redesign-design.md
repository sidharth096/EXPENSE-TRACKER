# Digital Wallet Redesign

## Direction

Restyle Khata as a modern digital wallet inspired by the supplied reference without copying its product identity or exact layout. The visual system uses pale ice-mint backgrounds, deep forest-green framing, bright lime highlights, rounded white modules, and a subtle pixel-grid motif.

## Product Hierarchy

The safe-to-spend amount remains the primary object. Its card receives the strongest color treatment and includes the daily allowance and planned-money breakdown. Spending categories, monthly planning, and recent transactions use quieter white or mint modules underneath it.

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop behaves as an expanded financial console with a persistent green sidebar and a balanced two-column dashboard. Mobile becomes a compact wallet interface with a green top bar, bottom navigation, denser cards, pill-like controls, and touch-friendly transaction rows.

## Visual System

- Page background: pale ice mint
- Primary frame: very dark forest green
- Highlight: electric yellow-green
- Cards: white with green-tinted borders and soft shadows
- Typography: rounded geometric sans for both headings and body
- Motif: restrained translucent pixel squares used only in the balance surface
- Motion: quick entrance and modal transitions with reduced-motion support

## Accessibility

Maintain visible focus states, high contrast text, minimum touch target sizes, keyboard modal dismissal, non-color labels for financial values, and responsive layouts without horizontal clipping.
